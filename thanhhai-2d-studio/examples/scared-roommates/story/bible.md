# Style Bible: Scared Roommates (White Cartoon 2D)

## Character Concept
Two minimalist white-headed cartoon roommates sitting on a living room sofa late at night.
- **Alex (Character A):** Large round ivory-white head, bold clean dark outline, big expressive circular eyes with anxious pupils, expressive mouth (screaming / trembling / relief).
- **Ben (Character B):** Slightly smaller round ivory-white head, nervous grimace, big trembling eyes with worry eyebrows.
- **Dual Lighting:** Warm amber rim light on the left (from the bedside/living room floor lamp) and cold moonlight cyan/white rim on the right (from the large moon window).

## Setting: Cozy Living Room at Night
- Slate purple/navy walls with framed mountain art.
- Left side: wooden shelf with books, hanging ivy plant, mini ghost figurine, warm floor lamp.
- Center: Slate blue/grey sofa, plush cushions, dark blue/teal checkered plaid blanket covering both roommates.
- Right side: Big nighttime window with starfield, glowing full moon, dark blue curtains.
- Foreground: Coffee table with red-striped popcorn bowl, TV remote, two cute gamer/skull mugs, corner of TV screen.
